# Material-specific distribution review - 3 October 2026

This review distinguishes observed source values, original statistical outputs, provider-licensed data and author code. It records evidence and packaging choices; it does not create provider permission or determine whether every individual factual value attracts a right.

**WDI:** the nine study indicators have recorded indicator-specific CC BY 4.0 evidence checked 2 October 2026. Current World Bank dataset terms were checked 3 October. Each included frozen extract is matched by SHA-256, archive member and recorded retrieval date in WDI_FROZEN_ATTRIBUTION.csv. No assessment-specific exception was found for these released HLO scores. Cite Angrist, Djankov, Goldberg and Patrinos (2021), Nature, doi:10.1038/s41586-021-03323-7. The released score inputs and study adaptations are included with catalogue attribution and the repository notice; no test items or proprietary assessment instruments are included.
